# OneDesign - Usage Guide

This guide covers sharing patterns and templates from the governing site with your brand sites. To install OneDesign and connect your sites, see the [Setup Guide](setup.md).

## Patterns

### Applying Patterns

1. On the governing site, head to `OneDesign > Pattern Library`.
2. In the 'Current Site Patterns' tab, use the search and the 'Pattern Categories' to find patterns, and click on the ones you want to select them.
3. Click on 'Apply to Sites', choose the brand sites in the 'Select Brand Sites' dialog, and click on 'Apply Patterns'.

Brand sites that already have every selected pattern can't be picked.

### Removing Patterns

1. In `OneDesign > Pattern Library`, open the brand site's tab to see the patterns applied to it.
2. Select the patterns you want to remove, and click on 'Remove Patterns'.
3. Confirm the removal. Editors on the brand site won't be able to insert those patterns any more.

## Templates

### Applying Templates

1. On the governing site, head to `OneDesign > Template Library`.
2. In the 'Current Site Templates' tab, select the templates you want to share.
3. Click on 'Apply to Sites', choose the brand sites in the 'Select Brand Sites' dialog, and click on 'Apply Templates'.

The patterns and template parts a template uses are created on the brand site along with it.

### Syncing Updated Templates

If you change a template on the governing site after applying it, open the brand site's tab in `OneDesign > Template Library` and click on 'Sync Shared Templates'. This sends the latest version of every template shared with that site, including its patterns and template parts.

### Removing Templates

1. In `OneDesign > Template Library`, open the brand site's tab to see the templates applied to it.
2. Select the templates you want to remove, and click on 'Remove Templates'.
3. Confirm the removal. Pages on the brand site that use those templates will switch to its theme's templates.
